ℹ What am I seeing?
Vehicle selector
Purpose: pick which dumper's driver view to show. Why: compare vehicles. How: reloads live telemetry for the chosen vehicle ID.
Status banner
Purpose: overall hazard level (SAFE / CAUTION / HIGH RISK / CRITICAL). Why: tells the driver at a glance whether to act. How: combines fog visibility, distance ahead, and time-to-collision.
Visibility meter
Purpose: shows current sight distance in metres. Why: low visibility means lower safe speed. How: read from the fog sensor for the truck's current zone.
Speed gauge
Purpose: compares current vs. recommended speed. Why: warns when driving faster than conditions allow. How: recommended speed is computed from visibility and following distance.
Distance / TTC tiles
Purpose: distance to the vehicle ahead and time-to-collision. Why: the earliest warning of a closing hazard. How: fused from radar, thermal, and V2V position reports.
V2V messages
Purpose: alerts shared directly by nearby trucks. Why: works even without GPS or control-centre connectivity. How: received over the truck-to-truck (V2V) radio link.
